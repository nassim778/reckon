Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy describes how the Reckon website handles information. Reckon is a free online calculator and does not require an account.

Calculator use

Arithmetic is performed in your browser. Numbers you enter into the calculator are not sent to Reckon by the calculator code, and the site does not currently include analytics or a contact form.

Hosting and fonts

The website is hosted by Vercel. When your browser requests the site, Vercel may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver, secure, and operate the service. See Vercel's Privacy Policy for details.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve those font files, and Google may receive technical information associated with that request. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Reckon may display ads provided by Google AdSense in the future. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to your settings, location, and applicable consent requirements. You can learn about how Google uses information on sites that use its services and manage ad personalization in My Ad Center. The calculator does not send the numbers you enter to advertising providers.

Your choices and contact

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect services provided by third parties. For questions about this policy or the site, contact us through the Reckon contact page.
