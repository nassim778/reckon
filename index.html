How to use the Reckon calculator

Reckon is a free, browser-based calculator for common arithmetic. Enter numbers with the on-screen buttons or your keyboard, choose an operation, enter the next number, and select equals to see the result. Use AC to clear and start again.

Available operations

  • Addition (+), subtraction (−), multiplication (×), and division (÷).
  • Percent (%) converts the current number to its decimal equivalent; for example, 25% becomes 0.25.
  • Change the sign with ± or enter a decimal with the decimal point.

Keyboard shortcuts

Use number keys and the decimal point to enter values. The +, −, *, and / keys select operations; Enter or = calculates, and Escape clears the calculator.

About results

Calculations are performed in your browser. Like most browser calculators, Reckon uses standard JavaScript number precision, so very large, very small, or repeating decimal results may be rounded for display. Do not use it where exact financial, medical, or other professional precision is required.
